Learnito Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026.

Learnito ("the app") is a study app for students. It is provided by Learnito ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, what stays on your phone, and how you can delete your data. Contact: puttupavankumar2002@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

When you create an account we collect:

  • Email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider, never in readable form).
  • Name, branch and semester that you enter, so the app can show your subjects.
  • Bookmarks of library notes that you save.

If you are an administrator, your account is also marked as an administrator.

2. What stays only on your phone

These are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our servers: PDFs you import, shelves and their order, highlights and page bookmarks you make inside PDFs, your reading position, your to-do tasks and calendar, CGPA calculator entries, how long you use the app and read each document (used only for your own dashboard), notes you download for offline reading, and your theme choice.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and we do not sell your personal data. We do not use analytics tools. The only third-party tracking-related technology in the app is the advertising described in section 5: the app shows ads, and Google AdMob collects the information listed there to serve them. We do not give AdMob your email address, name, branch, semester or bookmarks.

4. Why we use your data

Your account data is used only to run the app: to sign you in, show the right branch and semester, keep your bookmarks, and let administrators manage the library. We do not use it for advertising or profiling. The app is free and shows ads to cover its running costs; the data used for those ads is described in section 5.

5. Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in. Your account data is stored there and sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • File storage (Cloudflare R2). Library notes are PDF files that administrators upload to private Cloudflare R2 storage. When you open a note, your phone downloads it directly from Cloudflare using a short-lived link, and Cloudflare may receive technical information such as your IP address, as with any download. Some older notes may be hosted on services such as Google Drive.
  • Google AdMob (advertising). The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the app working, the SDK collects and sends to Google: your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), device and app information (such as device model, operating system version and app version), ad interactions (such as ads shown or tapped) and diagnostic information. This happens while the app is open. Google's use of this data is described in Google's advertising policy and Google's privacy policy.

Your choices about ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your Android settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone). If you delete the advertising ID, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised and the ID is not used. Uninstalling the app stops all ad data collection from the app.

6. Deleting your data

Full instructions: how to delete your account. In short, you can delete your account at any time inside the app: Profile → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, profile and bookmarks from our database and clears the notes, PDFs and tasks stored on your phone. You can also email puttupavankumar2002@gmail.com from your registered address and we will delete your account. Our hosting provider may keep encrypted backups for a short period before they are overwritten.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database access rules ensure that you can only read and change your own account data; only administrators can change library content. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password.

8. Children

Learnito is intended for students aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: puttupavankumar2002@gmail.com.

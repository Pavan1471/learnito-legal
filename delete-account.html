Delete your Learnito account

Applies to the Learnito app (package com.learnito.app), provided by Learnito.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Learnito app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete my account again.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't open the app, email puttupavankumar2002@gmail.com from the email address you registered with. Use the subject Delete my Learnito account. We will delete the account and reply to confirm, within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your sign-in (email address and password)Deleted permanently
Your profile (name, branch, semester)Deleted permanently
Your saved-note bookmarksDeleted permanently
Administrator status, if you had itDeleted permanently
PDFs, highlights, tasks, CGPA entries, study statistics and downloaded notes on your phoneCleared from your phone when you delete the account in the app. If you delete by email, uninstall the app or use Profile → Storage → Clear to remove them; they were never uploaded to us.

What is kept

Nothing about you is kept in our database after deletion. The shared library (notes, subjects and branches added by administrators) is not personal data and stays. Our hosting provider may keep encrypted backups for a short time, which are overwritten on the provider's normal schedule. We don't use them to restore deleted accounts.

See also the Privacy Policy.
